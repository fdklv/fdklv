# Hi, I'm Fedor 👋

I build **AI-powered automations and Telegram-first products**.

A few years ago I was selling electronics. Later I ran my own marketplace business on Wildberries and Ozon — and that is where I became obsessed with automation: too many repetitive processes, APIs, spreadsheets, edge cases, and things that simply shouldn't be done by hand.

Now I spend most of my time turning those kinds of problems into small, working systems.

### What I'm building now

- 🎬 **CINTO** — helps people decide what to watch without spending half the evening scrolling.
- 🗣️ **Frazio** — language learning in Telegram designed for people starting from zero.
- ⚙️ **Automations & integrations** — bots, workflows and API connections that remove repetitive work.

> I like products that feel simple on the outside and do the complicated work underneath.
